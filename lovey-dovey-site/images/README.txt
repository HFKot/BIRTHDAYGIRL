Put your photos in this folder to make the site yours.

Match the filenames used in index.html (inside the CONFIG block near
the top of the <script> section), for example:

  timeline-1.jpg, timeline-2.jpg, timeline-3.jpg, timeline-4.jpg, timeline-5.jpg
  photo1.jpg, photo2.jpg, photo3.jpg, photo4.jpg, photo5.jpg, photo6.jpg

You can also just rename your own photos to these names, or edit the
"image" fields in CONFIG to point to whatever filenames you use.

Until a photo is added, a soft placeholder shows automatically —
so the page always looks finished.
